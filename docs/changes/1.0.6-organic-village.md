# 1.0.6 — Organic rural settlement

Replaces the previous jittered residential lattice with 400 independently sampled
full-size homes, grouped around 17 uneven hamlet centres with scattered outliers.
Every house has a free rotation and a conservative non-overlapping collision
footprint. No row/column placement or reflected neighbourhood is used.

The former 34 parallel lanes are removed. A winding hillside spine and dog-leg
merchant approaches are terrain-conforming rotated ribbons, also drawn on the
expanded map. Twenty shops are independently distributed with distinct rotations;
forge wall collision bounds now support arbitrary angles. Existing PBR materials,
terrain slopes, NPCs and map UI are retained.

Combat-map buildings also use non-grid clustered placement and half the former
count: 23 open-world buildings, 4 forest buildings (rounded up from 7/2), 6 fire
buildings. Tests cover counts, deterministic reloads, geometry, free rotations,
clear house footprints, shop clearance and sloping surfaces. APK 1.0.6 is bundled
from current sources and passes ZIP, v1/v2 signature and aapt2 validation.
Physical-device and visual browser playtesting have not been performed.
