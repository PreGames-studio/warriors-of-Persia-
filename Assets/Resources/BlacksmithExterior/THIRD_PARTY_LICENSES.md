The downloaded glTF files were converted locally by `Tools/import_polyhaven_gltf.py` to OBJ and Unity Standard maps. ARM channels were explicitly unpacked: **R = AO, G = roughness, B = metallic**, with smoothness stored as `1 - roughness` in alpha. The conversion is documented in each model's adjacent `.json` file.

All architectural stone/wood/roof/metal/cobble and ground/detail maps are original procedural assets generated by the two scripts in `Tools/`; they are not downloaded imagery.
